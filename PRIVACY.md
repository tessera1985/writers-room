# Privacy

Writers' Room is a set of instructions for Claude. It has no code, no servers and no connectors.

- It doesn't collect, store or sell any data.
- It doesn't send anything anywhere. Your pages stay in your own conversation with Claude.
- The author never sees what you run through it.

What happens to your conversation itself is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy for the Claude app you're using.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/tessera1985/writers-room/issues.
